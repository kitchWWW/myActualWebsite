You Can Lose Your Balance

2025
a show


June 2025, performed by Brooklyn Motion Capture Dance Ensemble (BMCDE) at Mark Morris Dance Center

Suggested Listening:
  • 2 min, starting at 9:16
  • 2 min, starting at 20:39
  • 2 min jumping around later parts

“You Can Lose Your Balance” is an evening-length interdisciplinary work for eight performers, three technical personnel, and a large audience. Using a suite of live electronics powered by six capacitive touch sensors, it examines questions of community, collective action, and interconnectedness as the audience forms not only the set of the performance, but also the soundscape and connective tissue powering the entire event.

It was co-devised by Erin Landers and me, where I served as Artistic Director, Creative Coder, and Composer, and she served as Movement Director. Joe Tennis was heavily involved in early workshopping sessions, and Camilla Caldwell ran the live electronics during performance.

Contact Lite

2023
interactive chamber music



2023, performed by Third Coast Percussion and the Audience at Constellation Chicago

Suggested Listening:
  • (skip) audience instructions 0:00
  • 3 minutes at: 15:42
  • 2 minutes at: 20:24

In this work, the audience has been given a QR code to scan which turns their phone into a musical instrument that changes both sound and color based on where it is pointed. The instructions are then given to the audience via projector, who turn their phones until the color on the phone matches the color for your group on the projector.

To try out the phone electronics for yourself, scan here:

Be sure to:
  • Turn up your volume (media volume, if on android)
  • Click ON your side volume button, if on iPhone
  • When requested, hit “allow” orientation data, so we can turn your phone into an instrument!
For this project, I was composer and creative technologist, with Third Coast Percussion performing the music, providing audio samples I manipulated, and adapting my facilitation script to better fit their specific audience.
