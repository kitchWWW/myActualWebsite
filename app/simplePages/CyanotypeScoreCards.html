Want to perform this?
Please reach out and let me know! I will be happy to help in any way I can! If you could like a physical copy of the cards, I am happy to print, cut, and ship the collection to you at cost. This generally runs about $30USD because I make sure you get the ~high~ quality prints.

While the interpretation practices surrounding these cards have (intentionally) not been codified, a range of interpretive possibilities exist, including:
  • selecting a subset of cards to perform
  • drawing or dealing out cards, to improvise to live
  • asking the audience to choose cards for you to improvise and respond to
