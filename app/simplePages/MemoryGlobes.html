Memory Globe

2025
XR installation


This piece plays with representations of reality: In the VR headset, we swap back and forth between “real time pass through” (where we see Camilla playing violin in real time through the Quest’s onboard cameras) and “history” (where we see and hear a 360 degree video recording of Camilla made in the past, but at the exact same spot). By moving continuously between these two sensory inputs, we toy with perceptions of reality, memory, and sense of virtual space.
