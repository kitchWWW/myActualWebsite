where they fall

2026
zine duet


The score for this piece consists of a number of "chaos zines" - small folded instruction booklets that can unfold and refold together in a number of different fun and chaotic combinations. This allows a number of different instructions to appear next to one another at any given point in time, as the performers choose which panels to fold or unfold as the piece progresses. The evolution of the music is guided by an inherent knowledge latent within the paper, allowing for gradual transitions and oppositions to naturally flow from the physicality of the object presented.

Additionally, a similar zine is provided for the listener to encourage different modes of listening and engage them in the somatic process of the work. The PDF below includes sample zines for flute, voice, and listener, in that order.
