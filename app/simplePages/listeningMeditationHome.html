Listening Meditation for one’s Home

2025
zine




Listening Meditation for one’s Home is a self guided sonic experience for practitioners to embark on in their own homes. Housed in the format of a foldable zine, after entering through the front door of the paper home, the reader is guided to find a new spot in their dwelling to plant themselves and listen, look, and sense their past and present surroundings. Taking a journey through sonic and physical memory, this piece aims to recontextualize (and rebuild appreciation for) the spaces with which we are most intimately familiar.
