come, rest here

2026
hypercube ensemble


This piece explores what I call "shuffle music", a DAW-based sound collage practice where I take a field recording, drone, or other sound file, and I "shuffle" it via a custom-tuned algorithm, producing a textured wallpaper that both energizes and recedes.

I have previously explored this technique on a diptych album waiting // being (2025), which comprises two hour-long soundscapes. come, rest here distills this digital glitch soundscape technique to six minutes for live performers.
