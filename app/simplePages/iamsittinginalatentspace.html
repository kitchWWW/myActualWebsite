What happens when machines listen to themselves, think about what they are hearing, and try to re-emit those sounds? A mechanized attempt at deep listening.

This audio is generated by a series of calls to AI models. A prompt is first fed to the machine, in this case "a woman walking through a forest near the ocean". That prompt is then used to generate an audio file via Meta's AudioGen model, hosted on Hugging Face. That model is then captioned via whisper-small-audio-captioning, which turns the audio file back into a prompt. This prompt is fed into AudioGen, the output of which is fed back into the captioning model. Over and over, the cycle continues, as we witness the sonic explorations of AudioGen and whisper-small-audio-captioning, hand-in-hand.
